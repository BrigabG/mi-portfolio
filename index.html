About Me and Vision

Video games have been a fundamental part of my life since I can remember. Over the years, my passion for them has evolved from being just a player to becoming a creator, seeking to convey the emotions they can evoke. With this goal in mind, I decided to pursue a career in Video Game Development, which has become my greatest dream.

My main motivation is to share the same sensations and emotions I experienced as a player with future generations. I want to create experiences that leave a lasting impression, where every project is an opportunity to connect with people through unique stories and mechanics.
